# [ARDUINO]Sound Detector

## About

This is a sound detector made with the Arduino. The LED will light up when sound is heard.

## How to Use

First, download the Arduino IDE if you don't have it. Then, go to the file called "Sound_LED.ino" and copy and paste that into the Arduino IDE. The wiring is listed below with a diagram.

## Building

### Parts Needed

- Any Arduino board.
- A breadboard.
- A sound sensor.
- An LED(any color).
- 7 wires.

### Wiring

- Arduino board 5V connects to the red line on the side of the breadboard.
- Arduino board GND connects to the blue line on the side of the breadboard.
- Sound sensor +, V, or VCC connects to the red line on the side of the breadboard.
- Sound sensor -, G, or GND connects to the blue line on the side of the breadboard.
- Sound sensor A or A0 connects to analog pin 1 on the Arduino board.
- Sound sensor D or D0 stays unconnected.
- LED longer leg connects to digital pin 8 on the Arduino board.
- LED shorter leg connects to the blue line on the side of the breadboard.

### Labels

The red object in the top right corner is your sound sensor.

The green capsule object in the middle is a green LED(any LED color is fine).

The object in the top left corner is your Arduino board.

The object in the bottom left corner is your breadboard.
